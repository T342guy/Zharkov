Zharkov is an English-based Composed language designed to use the roots of the English language to its fullest, allowing those who already know English to learn Zharkov a little easier.

Zharkov was inspired by the raspiness of Russian, and the hostileness of German to create the work on progress language now! Though beware... Your throat might feel like you just swallowed a cheese grater.

## linguistics basics

### Vowels

Zharkov uses the standard set of english vowels, as an extention of its english roots.

- A 
- E
- I 
- O
- U
